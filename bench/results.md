### Fallback chain behaviour

- happy path: 1 call, no sleeping, no fallback
- 429 then 200: retried after the server's Retry-After (1000ms), no fallback
- Retry-After: 3600 capped to 2000ms — an invoice never hangs for an hour
- sustained 429: 3 attempts with backoff, then a real react-pdf invoice — the download never fails
- 401: one call, no backoff, straight to the fallback
- breaker trips after 3 failed invoices: later invoices skip the network, so an outage costs no added latency
- after the 60s cooldown the hosted renderer is retried and recovers on its own
